## Retrospective
Not held at the in-class session S30. Written up on 24 November.

**How we worked this sprint**
- Several long team calls to plan the product in detail: mapping the problem,
  designing the two-service architecture, dividing tasks between members and
  agreeing next steps. The result is the plan in [proposal.md](../proposal.md).
- 18 November: product idea confirmed (Job Application Tracker) and the
  repository created.

**What went wrong**
- Planning took much longer than expected because we wanted to perfect the idea
  and the architecture before writing any code. No code was committed until
  18 November, so the skeleton deploy could not happen in this sprint.
- The planning was done on calls and not written down as backlog items as we
  went, so the sprint left little visible evidence of the work.
- The team did not attend the Sprint 1 Review, so there was no feedback point.

**What we agreed to change**
- Time-box planning: stop designing once the plan is good enough to start, and
  improve it while building.
- Record decisions as backlog items and docs on the day they are made.
- Work in small, reviewed pull requests from everyone instead of waiting on one
  owner.
- Make a working deployed skeleton the first goal of Sprint 2, before features.
- Rotate the Product Owner and Scrum Master roles for Sprint 2.
